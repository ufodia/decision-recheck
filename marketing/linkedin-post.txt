Your product changed. Your buying advice, campaign copy, and support answers may still describe yesterday.

I built Decision Recheck to make that gap inspectable.

It is an open-source SaaS audit pack that connects three things: what someone needs, what a product promises, and what scoped checks actually show.

The included fictional case makes the problem concrete:

• Starter begins at $9/month with CSV export.
• Starter rises to $29. Export moves to the $49 Pro plan.
• The ad and help answer still describe the old offer.
• The buyer's budget is still $20/month.

One change now matters to three people.

The product owner needs a reproducible mismatch.
The buyer needs a clear fit decision.
Marketing and support need to know which statements to revisit.

Here is the useful part: correcting the copy does not make the new plan fit that buyer's budget. Those conclusions should stay separate.

Decision Recheck produces local HTML/JSON evidence bundles with linked artifacts, scoped findings, and three audience views. An explicit recheck compares the new evidence with a saved baseline. Blocked or untested work stays visible.

The demo runs without an API key. Optional model roles propose interpretations for human review; they do not predict real purchase behavior. FreshCtx supplies the dependency and freshness layer, and Playwright powers scoped browser checks.
